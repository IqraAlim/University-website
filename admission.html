Admission



Admission Requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Official transcripts from previous educational institutions
  • Standardized test scores (if applicable)
  • Letters of recommendation
  • Personal statement or essay
  • Application fee payment

Admission Timeline

Our admission timeline typically includes the following key dates:

  • Application opens: September 1st
  • Application deadline: January 15th
  • Notification of admission decisions: March 1st
  • Enrollment confirmation deadline: April 15th
